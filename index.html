About GetSorted

GetSorted keeps every recurring charge in one place, from streaming and music to AI tools and food memberships.

It forecasts what you'll pay over the next year, breaks spend down by category and flags renewals before they hit, so nothing charges you by surprise.

Built with JavaScript, Firebase Auth, Cloud Firestore and Chart.js.
